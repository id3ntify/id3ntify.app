The licence covers the **code**. It doesn't give you rights to the **ID3ntify name, the ID3ntify logo or the app icon**. These identify the official project by Elias Hajjar and are reserved:

- **Name:** as section 7(e) of the GPL allows, no rights are granted to use "ID3ntify" as a trade name or trademark.
- **Artwork:** the logo and icon files in `assets/` are not licensed under the GPL. They are © 2026 Elias Hajjar, all rights reserved, and are included so the official builds can use them.

This policy doesn't add any restriction to the GPL for the software itself. You can always use, change and share the code under the GPL. You just need to do it under your own name and icon.

## You can

- Say that your project is "based on ID3ntify" or "a fork of ID3ntify", with a link back to this repository.
- Use the name when writing or talking about ID3ntify, such as in reviews, tutorials and news.
- Build the unmodified source for your own use.

## Please don't

- Publish a modified version under the name ID3ntify, or under a confusingly similar name.
- Use the ID3ntify logo or icon for a modified version.
- Suggest that your version is official, or made or endorsed by Elias Hajjar.

## If you publish a fork

1. Change `APP_NAME`, `APP_ID`, `INSTALLER_GUID` and the links in `brand.py`.
2. Replace the files in `assets/` with your own icon.
3. Keep the existing copyright notices and the GPL licence, as the licence requires.
