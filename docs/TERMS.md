## 3. Your music files

ID3ntify can write tags and cover art, rename, move and organise files, and move or recycle duplicates. It only does this when you choose to, such as when you press **Write tags** or **Move to Recycle Bin**.

- **Matches can be wrong.** Recognition services and metadata sources sometimes return the wrong song, version, album or details. Check results before you write them, especially in bulk.
- **Changes can overwrite information.** Writing tags replaces the existing tags and cover art in the file. Renaming and organising change file names and locations.
- **Keep your own backups.** Back up your music before bulk operations. The optional backups, History and Revert features are there to help, but they may not be able to undo every change. Examples: if the backup was turned off, if files were changed again or moved elsewhere afterwards, or if the Recycle Bin has been emptied.

## 4. Third-party services

To identify songs, ID3ntify uses online services that are independent of this project, each with its own terms, privacy policy, limits and availability. They are only contacted when you start an identification (or check for updates, or use Plex). Nothing is uploaded in the background.

What leaves your computer depends on the service:

- **AcoustID** receives an audio fingerprint made on your computer (a numeric summary, not the audio) and the song's length.
- **Instant ID** uses a community-made, unofficial client for Shazam's recognition service. It sends a fingerprint made on your computer from about 12 seconds of audio. The audio itself and the filename are not sent. This service isn't provided, authorised or endorsed by Shazam or Apple, and may stop working at any time.
- **Preview check** sends the artist and title being checked to Deezer and Apple's iTunes search, and downloads the store's public 30-second preview to compare on your computer. Your own audio is not uploaded.
- **ACRCloud** and **AudD** (off unless you turn them on with your own account) receive a short audio clip, about 15 seconds.

Recognition accuracy, availability and continued access to any service can't be guaranteed. You're responsible for using them in line with their terms. Full details are in the Privacy policy.
